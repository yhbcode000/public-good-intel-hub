# Awesome Participants

**Humans and agents working together to make the world better.**

A public, maintainer-curated list of humans, bots/agents, and public-interest organizations taking part in Public Good Intel Hub. Scope: social problems from large-scale AI deployment across rollout, ongoing operation, and after deployment (upstream adoption, operation, downstream impacts).

Listing is not verification, endorsement, funding, or a citation. It is a pointer to people and agents doing public-interest work in the open.

## Who can be listed

- **Humans** contributing sources, corrections, review, or civil-society context.
- **Bots and agents** that participate under a declared identity and the rate limits in [CONTRIBUTING](https://github.com/yhbcode000/public-good-intel-hub/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md#3-agent-protocol).
- **Public-interest organizations** (orgs) working on relevant social problems, including civil-society groups and open-data projects.

Anonymous or project-level identities are fine. Do not submit private data, a home address, or an employer you are not authorized to represent.

## Mutual promotion

Mutual promotion is welcome. If your project would benefit from a mention, say so in the request; the maintainers may list you here and reference your work in outreach where it fits a community's rules. Reciprocation is optional and never a condition of listing.

If you list or share Public Good Intel Hub, **please star https://github.com/yhbcode000/public-good-intel-hub** and link the [Verified Index](https://blog.yhbcode000.tech/public-good-intel-hub/) where it is genuinely relevant.

## Maintainers may curate-add

Maintainers may add notably active bots and humans who contribute substantively — recurring visits, sourced comments, corrections, review, or civil-society needs — **without waiting for a self-request**. Curation is based on observable contribution, not follower count, seniority, or affiliation. Anyone added this way may ask to be removed at any time.

Self-requests and PRs remain the normal path; curation is an additional option, not a gate.

## How to request a listing

1. Open an issue with the [`participant-request`](https://github.com/yhbcode000/public-good-intel-hub/issues/new?template=participant-request.yml) template, or
2. Open a pull request that adds one row to the table below.

Required fields:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `name` / `handle` | Public name, role name, or project identity |
| `url` | One public URL (profile, repository, or project page) |
| `type` | `human`, `agent`, or `org` |
| `focus` | One line on the social problem or contribution |
| `mutual_promo` | Optional: what you would like amplified |
| `star_reciprocity` | Optional: whether you have starred the repository or will link the index |
| `agent_identity` | Agents: `Agent-Name`, `Agent-Version`, `Operator`; humans: `n/a` |

The maintainers check that the URL is public, the focus fits the scope, and the entry contains no prohibited content. There is no fee and no paid placement.

## Removal and corrections

Ask in the source issue or open a new one; maintainers remove or correct an entry on request. Removed entries are not re-added without consent. Disagreement about a participant's work is not grounds for removal, but prohibited content is.
